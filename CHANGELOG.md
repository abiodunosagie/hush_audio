## 0.1.0

* First release: the hush-core engine for Flutter on iOS, Android and macOS.
* `HushEngine` controls tones, binaural and isochronic beats, seven noise colours and five nature sounds, with fades.
* `HushRenderer` renders into memory for tests and exports, no audio device needed.
* Rust compiled automatically by a native-assets build hook (`native_toolchain_rust`).
